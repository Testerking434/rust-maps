# Russell Brunson — The Perfect Webinar

The most battle-tested long-form sales script in modern direct response. Used by Brunson, Hormozi, and thousands of operators to sell high-ticket info, coaching, SaaS, and services at scale.

## Part 1 — Overall structure (90 minutes)

```
[15 min]   INTRO + BIG PROMISE + ORIGIN STORY
[30 min]   TEACHING: The One Thing (vehicle) + 3 Secrets
[10 min]   TRANSITION: from content to pitch
[20 min]   THE STACK + THE OFFER + CLOSE
[15 min]   Q&A / reinforcement / second close
```

**Core rule:** content and pitch are NOT separate. The entire teaching exists to make the pitch inevitable.

## Part 2 — The Opening (15 min)

### 1. The Big Promise hook
First 60 seconds. One sentence.

> "In the next 90 minutes, I'm going to show you the exact [vehicle] that [specific result] without [common pain] — even if [common objection]."

Example: "I'm going to show you the exact XAUUSD trading system that produced 34% / year with under 12% drawdown — without martingale, without indicators, and without staring at charts all day, even if you've blown accounts before."

### 2. Who you are + quick credibility
30-60 seconds. Not a résumé — just why they should listen.
- One credential, one result, one reason you're qualified

### 3. The Origin Story (Epiphany Bridge)
Full 10-step bridge (see `brunson-hook-story-offer`). This is where trust is built. 8-12 minutes.

### 4. The promise stack
List 3-5 things they'll learn today. Each a "fascination" — curiosity-opening.
> "By the end of this, you'll know: (1) the one filter that keeps me out of news-event blowups, (2) the 3-word position-sizing rule that cut my drawdown by 60%, (3) why every guru's 'grid trading' works until it suddenly doesn't…"

## Part 3 — The One Thing (the vehicle)

Brunson's central move: teach them **one new vehicle/opportunity** they haven't considered.

**Rules:**
- It must be a **new opportunity**, not an improvement of an old one
- It must be **simple to understand** (one sentence)
- It must be **believable** (you provide proof)
- Your product is the vehicle or the fastest way to use it

Example: "ATR-Adaptive Position Sizing is a new category. Everyone else manages risk with fixed lots or martingale. I use volatility-adjusted sizing with news-event blackouts."

## Part 4 — The 3 Secrets (the belief-breaking engine)

Brunson's insight: **before someone buys, 3 beliefs must change.** The webinar breaks all 3.

### Secret 1 — The Vehicle belief
Break the belief that **your vehicle doesn't work** / that other vehicles are the answer.
- Teach something counter-intuitive about the vehicle
- Show proof it works (case studies, screenshots, equity curves)
- End: "This is why [new vehicle] is the only thing that actually works for [audience]."

### Secret 2 — The Internal belief
Break the belief that **"it won't work for ME"**.
- Agitate the internal doubt (not smart enough, not technical enough, not rich enough, started too late)
- Tell a story of someone exactly like them who succeeded
- End: "If [similar person] can do this, you can."

### Secret 3 — The External belief
Break the belief that **outside forces will stop them** (economy, age, family, time, competition, regulation, etc.).
- Name the external objection directly
- Show why it doesn't apply / how to get around it
- End: "The external thing you're worried about is actually an advantage."

**Teaching rule**: each Secret has one teaching point + one story + one proof element. 8-10 minutes per Secret.

## Part 5 — The Transition (10 min)

The single hardest moment in any webinar: going from "here's value" to "here's my offer" without losing the room.

**Brunson's transition script:**

> "Now, I could keep teaching you this for hours. But the truth is, knowing these 3 secrets isn't enough. You need a system to apply them. And that's what I want to talk to you about now.
>
> Because what I've just shown you is exactly what's inside [Product Name] — the system I've built to help [audience] [result] without [pain]. Let me walk you through exactly what's in it."

Now you're in the pitch. Smooth. No apology. No "sorry to pitch you" energy.

## Part 6 — The Stack & Close (20 min)

This is Brunson's most famous invention: **the Stack Slide**. Build perceived value through cumulative visual stacking.

### How the Stack works

Every time you reveal a bonus or component, add it to a visual list on screen with its standalone value. The list grows as you talk. By the end it's an intimidating pile of value.

```
Core Program: The Grand Slam Gold System          ($5,000 value)
Bonus 1: The 12-Week Trading Journal Template    ($497 value)
Bonus 2: Weekly Live Q&A Calls for 90 Days     ($2,000 value)
Bonus 3: Done-For-You News Calendar            (  $297 value)
Bonus 4: The Private Telegram Group            (  $997 value)
Bonus 5: Lifetime Software Updates             (  $997 value)
Bonus 6: Personal Account Review                ($1,500 value)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Total Value:                                   $11,288
Your Investment Today:                          $1,997
```

The visual gap between total value and price is the emotional weight. See `hormozi-offers` Part 3 for full stacking rules.

### The Stack slide rules

1. **Reveal bonuses one at a time** — each revealed with a short story about why it exists
2. **Each bonus solves a specific objection** (time, skill, fear, etc.)
3. **Dollar values are real** — what you'd actually charge if sold separately
4. **Total must be 5-10x the price** for maximum pull
5. **Show the Stack multiple times** during the close — repeat it every 3-4 minutes

## Part 7 — The 6 Closes (objection handling)

Brunson says most webinar buyers need to hear the offer **6 different ways**. Rotate through these closes in the final 20 minutes:

### Close 1 — The Direct Close
"Go to [URL] and enroll. Here's what you get. Here's the price. Here's the guarantee."

### Close 2 — The Future Pacing Close
"Imagine 6 months from now, after you've used this for 90 days. You're [new state]. Your account is [result]. Your days are [new state]. That's what this is."

### Close 3 — The Story Close
"One of my students, [Name], was exactly where you are. Here's what happened when they joined."

### Close 4 — The Objection-Destroyer Close
"You might be thinking [objection]. Let me address that directly. [Answer]."
- Do this for the top 3-5 objections
- "Too expensive" → ROI math + payment plan
- "Don't have time" → time-saving bonus + done-for-you option
- "Won't work for me" → case study with similar person
- "I can figure it out myself" → "you could, but here's what it costs in time + mistakes"

### Close 5 — The Urgency/Scarcity Close
"This price / this bonus / this cohort closes on [specific date]. After that, [real consequence]."
- Never fake scarcity. See `hormozi-offers` Part 5.

### Close 6 — The Final Call / "Walk Away" Close
"If this isn't for you, that's okay. Close the tab. No hard feelings. But if you're one of the people this IS for — stop waiting. Go to [URL] now."

The "walk away" move triggers reactance (they don't want to be rejected) and qualifies hard.

## Part 8 — The Q&A (15 min)

Every webinar ends with Q&A. Not for answering questions — for **closing the undecided**.

### Q&A rules
1. **Use planted questions** if you don't have enough live ones — questions that let you restate the offer or destroy an objection
2. **Every answer ends with a CTA back to the enrollment link**
3. **Address objections that came up in chat** ("Someone asked about X. Here's the answer.")
4. **Show the Stack Slide 2-3 more times during Q&A**
5. **Announce the deadline 3-4 times** during Q&A ("Cart closes in 47 minutes")

### The Second Close
During Q&A, deliver a **compressed second pitch** — shorter, sharper:
> "For those who just joined or are still deciding: here's what you get, here's the price, here's the guarantee, here's the deadline. Go to [URL]."

Most sales come in the final 15 minutes. Don't phone in Q&A.

## Part 9 — Post-webinar (the real money)

Webinar conversion rates: 3-10% on live attendees. The rest comes from follow-up.

### The 4-day email sequence after the webinar
- **Day 1**: Replay link + recap of the big promise
- **Day 2**: Case study / social proof + Stack Slide
- **Day 3**: Address top 3 objections + reminder of scarcity
- **Day 4 (last day)**: 3 emails — morning (cart closing today), afternoon (last 6 hours), evening (last hour)

Brunson says **50-70% of webinar sales** happen in the final 24 hours of the follow-up sequence. Cut the sequence short and you leave most of the revenue on the table.

## Part 10 — Audit checklist

- [ ] Big Promise in first 60 seconds?
- [ ] Epiphany Bridge origin story (full 10 steps)?
- [ ] One Thing clearly named as a new vehicle?
- [ ] All 3 Secrets teach + break a specific belief?
- [ ] Transition is confident, not apologetic?
- [ ] Stack Slide built cumulatively, not revealed all at once?
- [ ] Total value 5-10x the price?
- [ ] At least 4 of the 6 close types used?
- [ ] Real scarcity with a real deadline?
- [ ] Q&A used as second close, not just answering?
- [ ] 4-day follow-up sequence ready before webinar starts?

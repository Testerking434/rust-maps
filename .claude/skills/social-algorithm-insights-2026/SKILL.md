# Social Algorithm Insights 2026

Current as of April 2026. The fundamentals are stable — the tactics change every 3 months.

## The universal truth across all platforms

Every modern social platform ranks content on the same three signals, weighted differently:

1. **Average watch time / dwell time** — the single most important metric on every short-form platform
2. **Engagement-per-impression** (likes + comments + shares + saves per view)
3. **Completion rate** — did people watch to the end

Everything else (hashtags, posting time, captions) is noise compared to these three. If you optimize for these, the algorithm rewards you.

## TikTok algorithm (2026 state)

**Primary signals (in order of weight):**
1. **Rewatch rate** — people watching the same video ≥2 times → massive boost
2. **Completion rate** — finishing a ≥15s video
3. **Share rate** (especially DM shares to non-followers)
4. **Watch time on For You Page within first 2 hours** of posting
5. **Follower conversion rate** (followed after watching)
6. **Comments** (longer comments > short ones; replied-to comments > ignored)

**What kills reach:**
- **Swipes in <3 seconds** — death spiral. Your hook must grab attention before anyone's thumb moves
- **Watermarks from other platforms** (CapCut is fine, IG/YT watermarks are suppressed)
- **Low-resolution or letterboxed video** (9:16, 1080×1920 minimum)
- **Music with copyright flags** (use TikTok's native library, not uploaded songs)
- **Reposting same content** within 30 days

**2026 TikTok patterns that work:**
- **First 1.5 seconds** must be a pattern interrupt (fast movement, unexpected visual, bold text overlay)
- **Subtitles on** — 70%+ watch on mute initially
- **Text hooks** like "POV:", "Wait for it…", "I was today years old when…", "Nobody talks about this but…"
- **Loop back to the beginning** — 3-5 second videos that loop cleanly get double watch count
- **Native editing** (CapCut with TikTok export) performs better than Premiere Pro exports
- **Series format**: "Part 1" tags — 63% of Part 1 viewers watch Part 2+ (TikTok loves retention loops)
- **Trending sounds within first 3-5 days** of taking off, not peak

## Instagram Reels (2026)

**Primary signals:**
1. **Sends via DM** (most weighted of all signals — Adam Mosseri has said this publicly)
2. **Saves** (indicates the content has value)
3. **Watch time**
4. **Reach to non-followers**
5. **Profile visits after watching**
6. **Comments**

**2026 IG Reels patterns:**
- **Hook style that works**: Question → Answer, Problem → Solution, Bold Claim → Proof
- **Captions matter more than TikTok** — IG reads captions for topic classification
- **First 3 sends to DMs** from followers within 30 minutes is the biggest initial boost trigger
- **Carousel + Reel combo posts** outperform Reel-only for saves
- **Cross-posting from TikTok fails** (watermark detection) — always re-export clean

**What kills IG reach:**
- Telling people to "link in bio" kills reach (algorithm penalizes off-platform redirects)
- Posting more than 1 Reel per day (dilutes ranking signal across your account)
- Engagement bait ("comment YES!") — actively suppressed since 2024

## YouTube Shorts (2026)

**Primary signals:**
1. **Watch percentage** (% of video watched)
2. **Swipe-through rate** (higher = bad; people moving to next short before yours finishes)
3. **Likes per view**
4. **Subscribes after watching**
5. **Long-form channel correlation** — Shorts that feed into long-form videos get boosted

**2026 YT Shorts patterns:**
- **Optimal length**: 30-45 seconds (long enough for 80%+ retention to matter, short enough for multiple loops)
- **Hook the first 2 seconds or die** — YT's swipe threshold is shorter than TikTok's
- **Include visual payoff** in the final 3 seconds
- **Custom thumbnails for Shorts** (since 2025) — ~20% CTR boost
- **Chapter-style Shorts** ("3 reasons why…") outperform free-form
- **Feeding into main channel**: Shorts with a "watch full video" CTA to long-form convert best

**What kills YT Shorts reach:**
- Re-uploaded TikTok content — YouTube detects and suppresses
- Videos >60s — treated as regular video, different algo
- Shorts with no subscribers/engagement history on the channel (cold starts are brutal on YT)

## X / Twitter (2026)

**Primary signals:**
1. **Reply rate** (especially long replies with images/video)
2. **Bookmarks** (~3× the weight of likes since 2023)
3. **Time spent reading / expanding the post**
4. **Share to DMs**
5. **"For You" engagement ratio** (engagement from non-followers)

**2026 X patterns:**
- **First line / hook must stop the scroll** — you have ~0.8 seconds
- **Threads still outperform single tweets** for reach (avg ~5× reach)
- **Long-form (paid/verified)** 1000-2000 word posts get massive reach if well-written
- **Images with text > plain text > external links** (external links get ~50% suppression)
- **Don't edit posts in the first hour** — kills algorithmic momentum
- **Replies to large accounts** within 5 minutes of their post are a major discovery channel

## LinkedIn (2026)

**Primary signals:**
1. **Dwell time** on the post (hover + read time before scrolling away)
2. **Meaningful comments** (≥10 words)
3. **Re-shares with comment** (not empty re-shares)
4. **"People also viewed" correlation** — shared with your content cluster

**2026 LinkedIn patterns:**
- **"3-line hook" format**: Bold statement, space, space, then body (people click "See more" → dwell time boost)
- **Native documents (PDF carousels)** still outperform videos and single posts for dwell time
- **Personal stories with business lessons** > corporate announcements
- **No external links in post** — put them in first comment (~3× reach boost)
- **Post once per day max**, 4-5x per week optimal

## Hook engineering framework

Every viral short-form video is built on **3 acts in the first 3 seconds**:

1. **Pattern interrupt** (visual or auditory): unexpected movement, bold color, face close-up, loud sound
2. **Curiosity gap**: a question or incomplete thought that the brain needs to resolve
3. **Payoff promise**: implicit "keep watching and you'll find out X"

### Proven hook formulas (steal these)

- **POV hook**: "POV: you're the only one in the room who knows [unexpected fact]"
- **Contrarian**: "Everyone tells you X. They're wrong. Here's why."
- **Mystery**: "This is the most [superlative] [thing] and nobody is talking about it"
- **Before/After**: "I [did thing] for 30 days. Here's what happened."
- **Authority**: "I've [credential] for [years]. Here's the #1 thing I wish people knew."
- **Cliffhanger**: "Wait until you see what happens at 0:23…"
- **Stat shock**: "99% of people don't know this about [topic]"
- **Social proof**: "I tried the method [famous person] uses. It changed [thing]."
- **Problem-solution**: "If you struggle with X, watch this."
- **Question**: "Why do [group] always [behavior]? The answer surprised me."

## Retention engineering (short-form video)

The **retention curve** is your ranking. Flat or increasing = algo boost. Drop-off points = death.

**Common drop-off points and fixes:**

| Drop-off at | Fix |
|---|---|
| 0-2s | Better hook (see formulas above) |
| 3-5s | Transition too slow. Add a pattern break |
| Mid-video (10-15s) | Content plateau. Introduce a new idea or visual change every 3-5 seconds |
| Near end | Promise the payoff earlier, actually deliver it, loop back visually |

**Retention boosters that work:**
- **Captions that contradict the audio** (curiosity gap between hearing and reading)
- **Text that appears 1 word at a time** (eyes track it, brain can't disengage)
- **Visual change every 3-5 seconds** (cut, zoom, color shift, new angle)
- **"Wait for it" promises** with visible timer or countdown
- **Mid-video payoff preview** then full reveal at the end

## Growth tactics that still work in 2026

1. **Niche down, then niche down again** — "fitness" is dead, "fitness for new moms with back pain" is alive
2. **Series content** — Part 1/2/3 formats keep viewers engaged across multiple posts
3. **Cross-platform funnel** — TikTok → Instagram → YouTube long-form → email list
4. **Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes** — algorithmic boost
5. **Comment on large creators' posts** within 10 minutes of their upload — first-page visibility
6. **Duet/Stitch/Quote** established content with your own angle — free distribution
7. **Post at "cold start" hours for you** (when your followers are NOT online, algo pushes to For You)
